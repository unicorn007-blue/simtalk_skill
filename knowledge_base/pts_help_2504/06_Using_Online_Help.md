## Use the Plant Simulation Help

The Plant Simulation Help is fully integrated into Plant Simulation . You can always open the help by clicking the question mark button on the menu bar.

<!-- image -->

The Plant Simulation Help provides these topics in the following sections:

- What's New describes the new functions of the current and of the previous two releases. You can also open the description of the new functions by clicking the What's New tile on the Start Page .
- The Step-by-Step Help describes how to solve modeling tasks that you are facing in your everyday work day.
- The Reference Help describes the built-in objects.
- The 3D Reference Help describes the settings of the 3D component of Plant Simulation .

You can browse through the help with the previous page and next page buttons , just like you would leaf through a printed book.

## Search the Online Help

The Plant Simulation Help on Support Center provides a powerful search function for finding the information you are looking for. Everything that we describe below also applies to the installed version of the online help.

Depending on your search term, the number of results might sometimes be overwhelming though.

**Note**

If your search does not find what you are looking for, try a different spelling of the search term. Always type in as much of the expression your are looking for.

If camel case, for example createN* does not find createNestedList , try lower-casing the search term createn* .

In this example when search for Active . This resulted in a large number of results.

<!-- image -->

To refine searching you can:

**· Use quotation marks for an exact match**

To refine the search, enclose Active in quotation marks by typing in "Active" . The quotation marks signify that you want to search for an exact match, meaning that it finds all words of the search term in the order in which you type them in.

In our case, where the search term consisted of a single word, search found fewer results. It usually is a good idea to add the quotation marks to your search term.

<!-- image -->

If we are looking for part of a sentence though, the quotation marks do make a difference.

First we are searching for To activate the settings without quotation marks.

<!-- image -->

Then we are searching for "To activate the settings" with quotation marks.

<!-- image -->

Click the respective topic in the results list to open the page of that topic. The topic page itself does not highlight the search terms.

- Use [SimTalk] to find SimTalk functions

To search for any SimTalk function named Active , type in "Active [SimTalk]" . The results show all attributes named Active of all Plant Simulation objects.

<!-- image -->

Click the respective topic in the results list to open the page of that topic. The topic page itself does not highlight the search terms.

**· Use [check box], [text box], etc. to find UI items**

To search for a user-interface item, the check box in our example, type in "Active [check box]" . The results show all check boxes named Active of all Plant Simulation objects.

<!-- image -->

Click the respective topic in the results list to open the page of that topic. The topic page itself does not highlight the search terms.

**· Use [general description] to find the general description of a feature**

To search for the general description of a feature, Capacity in our example, type in "Capacity [general description]" . The result shows the topics describing what applies in general to the capacity of all Plant Simulation objects.

<!-- image -->

If we just enter "Capacity" search finds all topics referring to capacity. It is not guaranteed that the search term you entered is the first hit in the results list.

<!-- image -->

Click the respective topic in the results list to open the page of that topic. The topic page itself does not highlight the search terms.

**· Use the wildcard character * (asterisk) to match one or more characters**

To search for information with the wildcard *, type the asterisk * anywhere within the search term. In our example, we wanted to list all SimTalk functions starting with MeshMaterial , so we typed in MeshMaterial* .

The results show all attributes starting with MeshMaterial of all Plant Simulation objects.

<!-- image -->

Click the respective topic in the results list to open the page of that topic. The topic page itself does not highlight the search terms.

**Note**

If the search does not find the expression with the wildcard character, try a different spelling. Suppose you are looking for methods starting with moveto , so you type in moveto* . If the search result does not show the method you are looking for, try this spelling with camel case moveTo* .

**· Use OR to find either of the search terms**

To search for any of the specified words in the query, use OR . In our example, we wanted to list all topics that contain either the word move or the word TwoLaneTrack , so we typed in move OR TwoLaneTrack .

The results show all topics either containing move or TwoLaneTrack . The short section of the topic in the results pane does not necessarily show all of the search terms.

<!-- image -->

Click the respective topic in the results list to open the page of that topic. The topic page itself does not highlight the search terms.

**· Use AND to find all search terms**

To search for all of the specified words in the query, use AND . In our example, we wanted to list all topics that contain the word move and the word Container , so we typed in move AND Container .

The results show all topics containing move and Container . The short section of the topic in the results pane does not necessarily show all of the search terms.

<!-- image -->

Click the respective topic in the results list to open the page of that topic. The topic page itself does not highlight the search terms.

**Note**

Plant Simulation opens a new tab for each search result in the Web browser.

## Search for Special Characters

Follow the described instructions to search for special characters in the Plant Simulation Help .

The search for special characters in the Plant Simulation Help on Support Center might not return any results at all.

Special characters are, for example, @ (at sign), &amp; (ampersand), ? (question mark), * (asterisk), # (number sign, hash sign, or hash), ~ (tilde), [ (bracket), { (curly brace), := (assignment operator), the arithmetic operators , such as // (integer division), and the relational operators , such as = (equal to).

If the search for the special character does not return a result, try to spell out the respective character or character combinations.

Compare these examples.

To search for the special character @ (at sign), type in "at sign" .

**· Use "assignment operator" for :=**

## Install the Online Help Locally

How to install the Plant Simulation Help on your computer if you do not have a permanent internet connection at your place of work.

We provide the Plant Simulation Help on the Web by default. This way you always get the current state of the help with the newest descriptions and enhancements.

If you want to install the Plant Simulation Help on your computer and use it there without a connection to the internet:

- Consult the document Accessing the help from Plant Simulation on the download page ( https://support.sw.siemens.com/product/297028302/downloads ). It contains all you need for downloading and installing the help on your computer .

<!-- image -->

- Alternatively you can download the Siemens Documentation Server 3.0 or higher here https:// support.sw.siemens.com/product/266603595/downloads and install it on your computer.

<!-- image -->

You can also download and install the Documentation Proxy 3.2 .

The Documentation Proxy enables you to access product documentation on the Support Center without logging in or creating a Siemens ID account. This eliminates the need and overhead of installing documentation locally while still providing secure access.

- Alternatively you can download the Plant Simulation 2504 Help here https:// support.sw.siemens.com/product/297028302/downloads and install it on your computer.

<!-- image -->

**Note**

To install help content in a language other than English , you must first install the English help content.

- After you have installed the Siemens Documentation Server and the Plant Simulation 2504 Help , specify the Local Documentation Server on the tab General of the Preferences .

Specify http:/localhost:5000 if you installed the help server on your local machine with the default port. Plant Simulation automatically adds the identifier of the Plant Simulation user interface language you set.

**Note**

Be aware that the installed version displays the state of the help of the date when we created the help package, not the most up-to-date version that is available on the Web.

The help on the Web and the version installed on your computer work the same.

## Conventions in the Online Help

The descriptions in the Plant Simulation Help use typographical conventions to make reading them easier.

In the Plant Simulation Help we use these conventions:

- Within descriptions in the body text on the topic pages the names of objects begin with an upper-case letter and are italicized, for example ParallelStation , Station , etc.

In the Online Help , a term sometimes is a link to an object, for example ParallelStation . Click the link to open that topic in the Online Help .

- In the description in the Online Help the names of methods begin with a lower-case letter. Each new term after that begins with an upper-case letter. Methods are italicized, for example derive , updateDialog , etc.

In the Online Help , a term sometimes is a link to a method , for example derive [SimTalk] . Click the link to open that topic in the Online Help

- In the description in the Online Help the names of attributes and read-only attributes begin with an upper-case letter. Each new term after that begins with an upper-case letter as well. Attributes and read-only attributes are italicized, for example CreationTableActive , ReferenceTime , etc.

In the Online Help , a term sometimes is a link to an attribute , for example CreationTableActive [SimTalk] . Click the link to open that topic in the Online Help .

- In the description in the Online Help we use MU and part interchangeably, for example the processed MU and the processed part mean the same. If Part starts with an upper-case P though, it refers to the MU of type Part .
- In the description in the Online Help we use type in when you have to type a value into a text box or drop-down combo box.
- In the description in the Online Help we use specify when you have to specify a value, i.e., type in a value into a SimTalk attribute, method, read-only attribute, or function.

**Note**

These spelling conventions apply to the Online Help .

**Note**

SimTalk normally does not distinguish between upper- and lower-casing for the names of methods , attributes , read-only attributes , and functions , which you type into the source code of your Method [object] objects. You do not have to use the spelling, which the window Show Attributes and Methods shows. You can, for example, type in CreationTableActive , creationtableactive , or CREATIONTABLEACTIVE .
