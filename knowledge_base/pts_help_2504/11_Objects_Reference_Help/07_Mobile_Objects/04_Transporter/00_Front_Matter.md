### Transporter

Use the Transporter for modeling self-propelled vehicles allowing it to drive on its own on a Track or TwoLaneTrack or freely within the plant and to transport parts.

Icon

<!-- image -->

Description

The Transporter is an active, self-propelled mobile material flow object. It can:

- Drive on its own on the length-oriented objects Track and TwoLaneTrack driving forward or in reverse. It can load and transport Parts , Containers , and other Transporters .
