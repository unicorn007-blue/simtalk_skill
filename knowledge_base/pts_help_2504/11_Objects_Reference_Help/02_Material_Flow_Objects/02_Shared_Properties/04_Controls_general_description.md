#### Controls [general description]

You can define controls for all material flow objects on the tab Controls which a arriving MU triggers. When the MU triggers an internal sensor, the object triggers the Method you specified in as the respective control. These user-defined controls will then replace the built-in properties of the object.

<!-- image -->

For the length-oriented objects you can, in addition, define sensor controls anywhere on the object.

The Conveyor , Turntable , Track , TwoLaneTrack , and the Transporter activate the Methods you assigned to the sensor, when a MU passes the sensor. You can define and delete sensors in the dialog of the Conveyor , Turntable , Track , TwoLaneTrack on the Tab Controls &gt; Sensors or from within a Method object.

<!-- image -->

The Transporter provides the Sensors on the tab Load Bay .
