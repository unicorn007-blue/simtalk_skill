#### Change Settings with Mouse Wheel or Keyboard Keys

You can change settings in the dialogs of the objects with the mouse wheel or with keyboard keys in the text boxes on the tabs in the dialog.

- By default, the text boxes change the value in steps of 0.1 of the selected unit when you press the Up arrow or the Down arrow on the keyboard or roll the mouse wheel forward or backward.

<!-- image -->

When you specify an Angle , the Up arrow or the Down arrow or rolling the mouse wheel forward or backward change the value in steps of 1 degree.

<!-- image -->

- Holding down Shift changes the value in steps of 1.0 .

<!-- image -->

When you specify an Angle , holding down Shift and clicking the Up arrow or the Down arrow or rolling the mouse wheel forward or backward changes the value in steps of 10 degrees.

<!-- image -->

<!-- image -->

- Holding down Ctrl changes the value in steps of 0.01 .

<!-- image -->

<!-- image -->

When you specify an Angle , holding down Ctrl and clicking the Up arrow or the Down arrow or rolling the mouse wheel forward or backward changes the value in steps of 0.1 degrees.
