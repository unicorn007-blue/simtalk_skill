#### Tab Mezzanine Settings

##### Mezzanine Settings

Change the settings of the selected Mezzanine in your simulation model.

<!-- image -->

<!-- image -->

Here you can also select if the mezzanine is an Obstacle for the Worker or not.

<!-- image -->

If you cut out a piece of the railing, for example to add a stairway, you can restore the cut-out by clicking Restore Cutouts . This restores the state of the railing before you changed it.

To cut out a piece of the graphic, press the + key on the keyboard until only the piece of the graphic is selected that you want to cut. Press Del on the keyboard to delete that piece.

You can also edit the dimensions of the mezzanine by clicking Show Manipulators on the Edit ribbon tab. The mezzanine settings reflect this change.
