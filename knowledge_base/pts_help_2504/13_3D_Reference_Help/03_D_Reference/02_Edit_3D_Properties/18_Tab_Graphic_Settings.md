#### Tab Graphic Settings

Graphic Settings

Select if the selected graphic is an obstacle for the worker or not on the tab Graphic Settings .

**Note**

Only graphics, such as the graphic shapes Cone Frustum , Cuboid , Cylinder , Dimensioning , Sphere , and Text provide this tab.

In addition, the tab shows the Dimensions of the graphic. You can copy the individual values and paste them at another location where you need to use them.

Our sample cylinder has a Radius of 2 meters and a Height of 3 meters.

<!-- image -->

| Value   | Description                                                                                                      |
|---------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Width   | Shows the width of the graphic. The width is twice the radius.                                                   |
| X min   | Shows the minimum distance of the border of the graphic from the origin (zero point) of the scene on the x-axis. |

| Value   | Description                                                                                                      |
|---------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| X max   | Shows the maximum distance of the border of the graphic from the origin (zero point) of the scene on the x-axis. |
| Depth   | Shows the depth of the graphic. The depth is twice the radius.                                                   |
| Y min   | Shows the minimum distance of the border of the graphic from the origin (zero point) of the scene on the y-axis. |
| Y max   | Shows the maximum distance of the border of the graphic from the origin (zero point) of the scene on the y-axis. |
| Height  | Shows the height of the graphic.                                                                                 |
| Z min   | Shows the minimum distance of the floor of the graphic from the origin (zero point) of the scene on the z-axis.  |
| Z max   | Shows the maximum distance of the floor of the graphic from the origin (zero point) of the scene on the z-axis.  |

You can also view information about the Graphic Complexity of the graphic.
