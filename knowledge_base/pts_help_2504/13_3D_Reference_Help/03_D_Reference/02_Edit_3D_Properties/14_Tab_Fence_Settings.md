**Tab Fence Settings**

##### Fence Settings

Change the settings of the selected Fence in your simulation model.

<!-- image -->

You can also edit the dimensions of the fence by clicking Show Manipulators on the Edit ribbon tab. The fence settings reflect this change.

To make the fence an Obstacle for the Worker , select the respective entry from the list.

<!-- image -->

If you cut out a piece of the fence, you can restore the cut-out by clicking Restore Cutouts . This restores the state of the fence before you changed it.

To cut out a piece of the graphic, press the + key on the keyboard until only the piece of the graphic is selected that you want to cut. Press Del on the keyboard to delete that piece.

You can set one of the dimensions to 0 by dragging the respective manipulator.
