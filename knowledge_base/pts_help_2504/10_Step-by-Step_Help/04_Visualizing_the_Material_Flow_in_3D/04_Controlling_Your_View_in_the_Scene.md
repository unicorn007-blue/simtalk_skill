### Controlling Your View in the Scene

#### Control Your View

The 3D window provides several ways of setting how you look at the 3D scene, i.e., of controlling the camera that represents the eyes of the viewer looking at the scene.

You can:

- Set the Main Directions
- Set View Points
- Attach a Camera to an Object/Frame and Detach it
- Animate the Camera

Back to Visualizing the Material Flow in 3D

#### Set the Main Directions

Independent of the active camera setting, you can adjust the view to any main direction of the view or of an object at any time. When you selected an object, the 3D window zooms out this object and adapts the direction of the camera to the selected main direction of the coordinate system of the object.

#### Attach a Camera to an Object/Frame and Detach it

You can attach a camera to any object, so that you can view the scene through the lens of this object, and detach a camera from it again, when you do not need it any more. When the object moves through the scene, for example because you started the simulation, the camera will move along with the object you attached it to.

<!-- image -->

In addition, Plant Simulation :

- Visualizes the camera you attached, i.e., inserts an animatable object that represents the camera.
- Enables you to change between object view and normal view .
- Enables you to manually transform the camera you attached: By changing the view while in object view, or by manipulating the object, while in normal view.
- Enables you to set the speed and the direction of the animation.
- Enables you to pause, continue, and stop the camera animation independent of other animations and simulations.

#### Animate the Camera

You can make the camera move on any animation path of the selected object or of the root object of the scene. Here, you fly on this path and view the scene from it.

<!-- image -->

In addition, Plant Simulation :

- Visualizes the animated camera, i.e., inserts an animatable object that represents the camera.
- Enables you to change between animation view and normal view during the animation.
- Enables you to manually transform the animated camera: By changing the view while in animation view, or by manipulating the object, while in normal view.
- Enables you to define the speed of the animation and the direction of the animation.
- Enables you to pause, continue and stop the camera animation, independent of other animations and simulations.
