### Recording a Video

#### Record a Video

You can record a video of the crucial parts of your simulation model. This way, you can pass the video on to anybody or publish it on the Intranet or the Internet, without the person viewing the video having to install Plant Simulation on his computer. The recorder creates an AVI file that any multimedia player can play.

The Video Ribbon Tab provides all tools for recording and playing the video.

#### Select Video Settings

After you prepared the scene, you can:

<!-- image -->

<!-- image -->

- Click Start Simple Recording on the ribbon tab Video and select the settings to record a video of Plant Simulation or the active 3D window.

The video will be recorded in real-time, meaning that recording a minute of physical real time corresponds to a minute of playback time in the video. You might, for example, use this type of video recording to demonstrate how to operate a model.

<!-- image -->

<!-- image -->

- Click Start Simulation Recording on the ribbon tab Video and select the settings to record a video of a simulation. The simulation does not have to run beforehand.

The video records in scaled simulation time, meaning that the number of minutes of simulation time, which you entered as Real-time x [video] , corresponds to one minute of playback time of the video. You can, for example, use this type of video recording to play the simulated production with realistic time behavior.

<!-- image -->

**Note**

To prevent that Plant Simulation overwrites the last recorded AVI file, rename the output file before recording another video.

#### Set Up the Scene for Recording

There are a number of items to keep in mind before you start recording the scene:

- Drag the scene window to a size that shows all that you want to show to the viewer, but not more, unnecessary information.

**Note**

If you would like to record a video in the display resolutions 720p or 1080p, you can set the size of the window to be recorded to these sizes in the dialogs Start Simple Recording and Start Simulation Recording .

The size of the scene determines the size of the video, a large scene uses more processor time and might take a long time to animate and creates a larger video file.

- Decide from which position and which angle you want to view and record the scene. Then, rotate and zoom the scene accordingly before starting the simulation.

**Note**

If you are recording a simulation video, you can automatically connect the recording with playing a camera animation by selecting Play Camera Path .

#### Play the Video

After you have recorded the video, you can play it the way you recorded it. Or you can edit it with a digital video application, such as Adobe Premiere, Camtasia, etc.

<!-- image -->

- To play the video, click on the Video ribbon tab.
- The default player you set in MS Windows then plays the video.
