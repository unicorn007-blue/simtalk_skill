#### Activate 3D

Activates or deactivates 3D .

<!-- image -->

**Note**

The command Activate 3D is only available in a 2D Only model .

After you started 3D , the 2D Frame window shows the x-axis and the y-axis in red. The point where these two red lines intersect is the point, which will be mapped to the zero point, i.e., the Position of the grid, in 3D.

You can set the origin of the grid in the Frame in 2D with the attribute AxesOrigin .

<!-- image -->

#### Open Frame in 2D/3D

Opens the window of the active Frame of the current object in a 3D window .

<!-- image -->

Note

Open Frame in 2D/3D is only required for the setting 2D and 3D to transform a 2D Only model into a 3D Only model .

#### Show Copilot

Shows or hides the Copilot window. The Copilot is an intelligent assistant designed to help you to quickly find the information you need in the Plant Simulation Help .

<!-- image -->

**Remarks**

The Copilot is only available in Plant Simulation X .

Copilot Sign In

To sign in to your Siemens account click Sign In .

<!-- image -->

This opens a webpage on which you type in your E-mail address used for authenticating you.

<!-- image -->

<!-- image -->

**Description**

The Copilot is an intelligent assistant designed to help you to quickly find the information you need in the Plant Simulation Help . Simply ask your question, and Copilot will efficiently search through the content of the help to provide the most relevant answers, streamlining your support experience. This assists you in finding the solutions you are looking for much faster than searching the help yourself. Be aware though, that AI generated content may contain inaccuracies.

Type your question into the prompt field. You can type in up to 2000 characters.

<!-- image -->

In our example we wanted to know how to create a tugger train in Plant Simulation , so we typed in How do I create a tugger train in Plant Simulation? and then click Send .

<!-- image -->

Copilot answers our question as follows:

- It provides a step-by-step instruction .
- It lists the help topics it used as the Sources for answering the question. Click on a link to open the respective help topic.
- It shows Related Questions providing additional information.

#### Close All Windows

Closes all open object windows.

<!-- image -->

Object windows are the windows of the Frame , the Method , the Method Debugger , the DataList , the DataQueue , the DataStack , the DataTable , and the Icon Editor .

Arrange Icons [program window]

##### List of Open Windows

Shows the list of the currently open object windows.

The list of open windows lists 9 currently open object windows of the Frame , the Method , the Method Debugger , the DataQueue , the DataStack , the DataList , the DataTable , and the Icon Editor . It also shows open 3D windows. Plant Simulation always opens object windows in the background. To select the window of one of these objects and to bring it to the foreground, click it in the list. It then shows a check mark to the left.

<!-- image -->

To bring an open object window to the foreground, you can also click the icon of the window in the window tab bar .
