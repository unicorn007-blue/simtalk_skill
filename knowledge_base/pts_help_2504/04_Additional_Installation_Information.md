### Plant Simulation Professional License

The Plant Simulation Professional license is the most powerful program package within the Plant Simulation product family and enables you to run the most complex simulation studies.

The number of objects is unlimited. Numerous mechanisms support creating, validating, and maintaining large projects. This license enables you to interactively create simulation models using the built-in class objects of the Material Flow Objects , the Fluid Objects , the Resource Objects , the Information Flow Objects , the Display and User Interface Objects , the Mobile Units [MUs] , and the built-in Tools . You can use the built-in objects as a starting point to create customized objects that meet your exact requirements by employing the programming language SimTalk .

This license also provides a number of features for the advanced user. These features facilitate editing complex projects, increase efficiency, and support collaboration between colleagues within a project.

With the Plant Simulation Professional license you can:

- Interactively model with the built-in objects and user-defined objects, controls, and processes with any number of class objects.
- Insert an unlimited number of objects.
- Save the model as a folder model which facilitates storing it in a version control system such as Git .
- Animate the material flow during the simulation runs in 2D or in 3D.

- Use Plant Simulation VR .
- Access tables and lists at any time during the simulation runs.
- Modify, adjust, or extend optional libraries as well as work with interactive communication interfaces and with user-defined dialogs .
- Modify the simulation model and its parameters during the simulation run interactively or via fi le interfaces , databases , or binding of Socket interfaces .
- Evaluate and display model sizes with the built-in analysis and evaluation tools .
- Access the built-in objects for the material flow and the information flow, as well as other objects for controlling, managing, and visualizing your model.
- Use the Profiler for analyzing time usage of the methods contained in the model, allowing you to optimize the SimTalk source code within.
- Use the objects AttributeExplorer , Data Fit , Dialog , Experiment Manager , FlowControl , Generator , TimeSequence , and Trigger .
- Use the integrated interfaces: COM , DWG , DGN and DXF Reader , HTML Interface , and XML Interface .
- Use the built-in merge-mechanism for updating classes in the class library in existing models.
- Use the HTML integration for standard browsers with access to all Plant Simulation objects for fast visualization of data and simulation results.
- Use the genetic algorithms to optimize your simulation model.

### Plant Simulation Standard License

The Plant Simulation Standard license covers the better part of typical simulation studies and enables handling even complex modeling tasks.

The Plant Simulation Standard license provides object-orientation, hierarchy, openness for database access, and interactive modeling during the simulation run. In addition, it supports numerous mechanisms for creating, validating, and maintaining large projects.

The Plant Simulation Standard license enables you to interactively create simulation models using these built-in objects:

- Material Flow objects : Connector , EventController , Frame , Interface , Source , Drain , Station , ParallelStation , AssemblyStation , DismantleStation , PickAndPlace robot , Store , PlaceBuffer , Buffer , Sorter , Conveyor , AngularConverter , Converter , Turntable , Turnplate , Track , TwoLaneTrack , and Cycle .
- Fluid objects : Pipe , FluidSource , FluidDrain , Tank , Mixer , ContinuousMixer , Portioner , DePortioner , and MaterialsTable .
- Resource objects : Workplace , Footpath , WorkerPool , Worker , AGVPool , Marker , Exporter , Broker , ShiftCalendar , and LockoutZone .
- Information Flow objects : Method , Variable , DataTable , FileInterface , and FileLink .

- User Interface objects : Comment , Display , Chart , Button , Checkbox , and Drop-Down List .
- Mobile objects : Part , Container , and Transporter .
- Tools : Bottleneck Analyzer and Layout Optimizer .
- Libraries and modeled objects : High Bay Warehouse and Cranes and More .

Plant Simulation determines the number of objects in the Standard licenses the same way as in the Foundation and Student license .

It counts the number of objects in the simulation Frame and not in the entire model.

With the Plant Simulation Standard license you can:

- Interactively model with the built-in objects and with user-defined objects, controls, and processes with up to 4000 objects. DataTables and control logics are not included in this number.
- Modify, adjust, and continue modeling with add-in libraries as well as work with interactive communications.
- Access tables and lists during the simulation runs.
- Modify the simulation model and its parameters during a simulation interactively or via the FileInterface (.txt, .xls) or the COM interface .
- Animate the material flow during the simulation runs in 2D or in 3D.
- Evaluate and display model sizes with the built-in analysis and evaluation tools.
- Use the built-in material flow objects and information flow objects, as well as objects for controlling, managing, and visualizing your model.

The Plant Simulation Application license enables you to interactively create simulation models using customized objects from object libraries as well as using these built-in objects:

- Material Flow objects : Connector , EventController , Frame , Interface , Source , Drain , Station , ParallelStation , AssemblyStation , DismantleStation , PickAndPlace robot , Store , PlaceBuffer , Buffer , Sorter , Conveyor , AngularConverter , Converter , Turntable , Turnplate , Track , TwoLaneTrack , and Cycle .

This license can visualize Plant Simulation models in 2D as well as in 3D. The programming language SimTalk is not available in the Plant Simulation Application license .

With the Plant Simulation Application license you can:

- Model interactively and enter and select settings of the class objects in the dialogs of the objects.
- Create models with application-specific user-defined objects, as well as access the built-in objects that are part of the user-defined objects.
- Execute simulation runs with 2D and 3D graphics and animate simulation models, record statistics, analyze simulation runs, and save the results and the statistics tables.
- Optimize the simulation results by modifying the model parameters with file interfaces or databases.
- Automatically optimize the simulation model with genetic algorithms .
- Use pre-defined application objects from object libraries available for many industries.

### Plant Simulation Runtime License

The Plant Simulation Runtime license enables you to interactively modify model parameters and to execute simulation runs within Plant Simulation models.

With a Plant Simulation Runtime license you can neither insert any of the objects built into Plant Simulation nor can you insert user-defined objects, nor can you use the programming language SimTalk .

The Plant Simulation Runtime license includes the optional add-ins of the interface package and GanttChart .

With the Plant Simulation Runtime license you can:

- Interactively set the parameters of the class objects in the dialogs as well as restricted access to the built-in objects contained in the user-defined objects, for example lists and tables .
- Execute simulation runs with 2D and 3D graphics and animate simulation models, record statistics, analyze simulation runs, and save the results and the statistics tables.
- Optimize the simulation results by modifying model parameters via file interfaces, databases, or binding of ActiveX , COM , C-Programming , ODBC , Socket , and ORACLE SQL interfaces .
- Automatically optimize the simulation model with Genetic Algorithms .
- Display the results in GanttCharts according to resources and orders.

**Note**

You cannot create new simulation models with the Plant Simulation Runtime license .

### Plant Simulation Simulation License

The Plant Simulation Simulation License supports running complex simulation studies.

The Plant Simulation Simulation license enables you to interactively create simulation models using these built-in objects:

- Material Flow objects : Connector , EventController , Frame , Interface , Source , Drain , Station , ParallelStation , AssemblyStation , DismantleStation , PickAndPlace robot , Store , PlaceBuffer , Buffer , Sorter , Conveyor , AngularConverter , Converter , Turntable , Turnplate , Track , TwoLaneTrack , and Cycle .
- Fluid objects : Pipe , FluidSource , FluidDrain , Tank , Mixer , ContinuousMixer , Portioner , DePortioner , and MaterialsTable .
- Resource objects : Workplace , Footpath , WorkerPool , Worker , AGVPool , Marker , Exporter , Broker , ShiftCalendar , and LockoutZone .
- Information Flow objects : Method , Variable , DataTable , FileInterface , and FileLink .
- User Interface objects : Comment , Display , Chart , Button , Checkbox , and Drop-Down List .
- Mobile objects : Part , Container , and Transporter .
- Tools : Bottleneck Analyzer and Layout Optimizer .
- Libraries and modeled objects : High Bay Warehouse and Cranes and More .

This license is especially suited for using the distributed simulation , for using neural networks , and for the optimization tools , such as genetic algorithms . Using several simulation licenses enables you to run experiments with different parameters, control logics, random number streams, or layouts in parallel to arrive at results considerably faster. The Plant Simulation Simulation License does not have a graphical user interface. You can neither visualize the simulation model nor manually change the model.

The number of objects within the model is unlimited. The Plant Simulation Simulation License contains the Plant Simulation interfaces ActiveX , COM , ODBC , SQL for Oracle , Socket , and XML . You can also run models that contain the GanttChart , but you cannot visualize them with a simulation license.

With the Plant Simulation Simulation License you can:

- Modify the model and its parameters during the simulation run via interfaces.
- Execute simulation runs without a graphical user interface, visualization, or modeling.
- View and save the results of the simulation runs.
- Use the integrated interfaces ActiveX , COM , ODBC , SQL for Oracle , Socket , and XML Interface .

- Use all libraries that do not require a license.

### Plant Simulation Viewer License

The Plant Simulation Viewer license enables you to run and visualize simulation models, but you cannot change the model or its parameters.

The programming language SimTalk is deactivated in the Plant Simulation Viewer license . You cannot import and export data, and you cannot communicate with other applications. To run simulations with the Plant Simulation Viewer license all required input data has to be part of the simulation model!

The Plant Simulation Viewer license enables you to:

- Visualize built-in and user-defined objects with integrated parameters.
- Visualize values in lists and tables .
- Execute simulation runs with 2D and 3D graphics, animate simulation models, record data, and evaluate simulation runs.
- Use Genetic Algorithms for the automated optimization of your simulation models.
- Use an unlimited number of objects.
- Provide an integrated presentation of the results in GanttCharts depending on resources and orders.

These Plant Simulation options are available for various Plant Simulation applications.

- Plant Simulation Interface Package

- Plant Simulation Omniverse Connector

### Plant Simulation Interface Package

The Plant Simulation Interface Package provides a number of interfaces to external programs.

- You can exchange data with databases and other applications
- You can control Plant Simulation from other applications or vice versa

### Plant Simulation Gantt Chart

The Plant Simulation Interface Package provides these interfaces:

- The ActiveX ® interface to work with ActiveX controls
- The C -programming interface to call C functions from within Plant Simulation
- The MQTT interface to transmit messages from device to device over the Internet
- The ODBC interface to connect to databases and applications which support ODBC drivers
- The OPCClassic interface to connect to an OPC server to exchange signals with PLCs
- The OPCUA interface to read and write control variables and signals of PLC controls
- The Oracle11g/Oracle19c interface to exchange data with Oracle databases via an Oracle client
- The PLCSIM\_Advanced interface to exchange signals and data between Plant Simulation and virtual PLCs
- The SIMIT interface to connect to process control devices, such as PLC controls, via SIMIT (Simulation &amp; Testing)
- The Socket interface to exchange data with other applications via a TCP/IP connection

**Plant Simulation Gantt Chart**

Plant Simulation Gantt Chart is a program for visualizing Gantt charts , for example, production workflows.

The GanttChart can visualize data from a simulation run. In addition to the mere occupancy data, the GanttChart can also display failures of machines, pausing times, and blocking times.

This way the GanttChart immediately shows which machines or stations are overloaded or have a low utilization and which production orders or products have long waiting times during the production process.

### Plant Simulation Omniverse Connector

The Omniverse Connector is a powerful add-on application that helps you transfer your geometries and simulation studies from Plant Simulation into NVIDIA Omniverse to realize an industrial metaverse visualization, simulation, and collaboration experience.

## Trust-ID to Permit Models Access to the Computer

Plant Simulation automatically saves the trust ID in a text file named TrustID.txt in the directory C:\Users\&lt;username&gt;\AppData\Roaming\Siemens\Tecnomatix Plant Simulation on your computer.

All versions of Plant Simulation installed on this computer share this trust ID to permit models access to your computer.

When you replace your old computer with a new computer, copy the file TrustID.txt to your new computer if you did not install any version of Plant Simulation on your new computer yet. Otherwise, Plant Simulation would generate a new trust ID. Then, Plant Simulation would consider your existing models not as your own models and would not automatically permit them access to the computer.

## Installing Product Updates

You can install the most recent product update for Plant Simulation by selecting File &gt; Help &gt; Check for Update . Plant Simulation also recognizes that a new version is available when you start it.

- Select File &gt; Help &gt; Check for Update .

<!-- image -->

- Type in your Siemens ID Username and your Siemens ID Password to log on to the GTAC site. You will typically use the Siemens ID Access Code that the About Plant Simulation dialog shows.
- Download the Plant Simulation product update.
- Double-click the executable to start the installation.

**Note**

To run a silent installation, i.e., to install the product update without having to respond to dialogs or messages, open a Command Prompt window and start the downloaded executable with the command-line parameter /s , for example PlantSimulation\_2504.0005\_Update.exe /s .

**The product update uses the registry value**

HKEY\_LOCAL\_MACHINE\SOFTWARE\Siemens\Tecnomatix Plant Simulation 2504\ (Default) to determine the installation folder. If this registry value does not exist, you can specify the folder with the following command-line parameters: /s "C:\My Plant Simulation Folder" .

**Note**

The description above does not apply when you Use Cloud Licensing .

Your cloud licenses are provided and managed by Siemens, see Install Plant Simulation X from Siemens Software Center .

## Opening a Crashed Model

If Plant Simulation terminates unexpectedly, it saves the model to the Temp folder of your computer's hard disk drive with the name Plant Simulation.&lt;your login name.bak&gt; , for example Plant Simulation.tom.bak .

After restarting, Plant Simulation opens a dialog. Click Yes if you want Plant Simulation to try to open this model.

**Note**

We recommend not continuing to work with the crashed simulation model, but to go back to the last saved version before the crash.

We recommend saving your model often.
